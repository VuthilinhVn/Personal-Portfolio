Analysis and Recommendation for FPT DRM Key Budget

Objective:

FPT currently owns approximately 380,000 DRM keys for various content packages including SVOD, TVOD, AVOD, K+, and Special. However, this number may not align with actual demand, leading to either wasted costs or lost revenue. This project analyzes usage data over a 60-day period from April 3, 2020, to June 1, 2026, to recommend the optimal number of DRM keys FPT should maintain to maximize cost efficiency and revenue.
